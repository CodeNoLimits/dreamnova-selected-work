04 · Built for 40–60 Min YouTube Episodes

How We Edit Your Long-Form Videos.

A streamlined, reliable production pipeline designed to keep your raw field energy intact while maximizing viewer retention.

STEP 01 · 72H TURNAROUND

First Cut in 72 Hours

Drop your raw camera files (40–60+ min explorations, vlogs, or interviews) in Google Drive. You get a complete, structured first cut within 72 hours.

STEP 02 · RETENTION PACING

Hook & Story Flow

We pull the strongest 15–20s moment to the cold open, trim dead air and repetitive walking segments in natural silence, and pace chapters so viewers stay through the hour.

STEP 03 · CAPTIONS & CONTEXT

Dynamic Subtitles & Graphics

Word-accurate captions for indistinct field dialogue, clean chapter markers, and subtle location/context callouts—plus a separate .SRT file for YouTube.

STEP 04 · COLOR & AUDIO

Audio Cleanup & Color Grade

Wind & harsh background noise reduction, dialogue leveling, subtle music ducking, consistent outdoor/indoor color balance, and −14 LUFS YouTube mastering.

STEP 05 · THUMBNAILS & SHORTS

Thumbnails & Vertical Cuts

High-contrast custom thumbnails + YouTube chapter timestamps, plus vertical Shorts extracted from the best moments to drive traffic to the full video.
